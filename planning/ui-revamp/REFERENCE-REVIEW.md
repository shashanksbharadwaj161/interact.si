### Browser observations supplementing the extracted reports

- **Hitodama https://hitodama.in/** loaded successfully in the browser, although the text-fetch tool failed. Inspected the live top, workflow section and circle-to-ask preview. Cream/pale paper background, confident serif headline with blue emphasis, one flame identity, simple pill navigation and a framed Windows workflow demo. Lower workflow demonstrations have legible spatial grouping. Useful lesson: focused identity and task storytelling, not its flame/brand/copy. Marketing demos do not verify its installed app.
- **Lapa's exact ecommerce motion page** loaded in the browser despite text-fetch failure. This establishes the catalogue is accessible, not that every linked recording was watched.
- **Glass Portfolio** rendered an embedded white composition with materially distinct reflective orbs around the central text. Useful material study; no paid source acquired.
- **Supaste** showed a blue-to-cyan product hero, strong type hierarchy and a compact navigation. Borrow hierarchy/compact utility patterns rather than its full marketing gradient.
- **Basement** showed a large black/white Showcase title and high-contrast project grid. Useful deliberate scale/contrast, unsuitable as a literal daily-work screen.
- **Unicorn** rendered an atmospheric purple/blue shader region beneath a concise heading. The technique is inspiration for an isolated companion surface, not permission to give the whole app a purple shader backdrop.
- **Zajno** initially presented a minimal dot/loading scene. Described motion chapters are text-verified; complete interactive choreography was not observed. Do not claim timings or all demonstrations were tested.
- **Space UI Bloop** initially loaded, then rendered a blue/porcelain material orb with Idle/Listen/Think/Speak controls and a configuration panel. Static appearance was visually reviewed. Controls describe demo states; no actual microphone input or audio accuracy was tested, and its source was not copied.
- **Skecher** presented a dark, serif-led component-library hero with a rendered texture scene. Planes initially presented a largely dark media/loading scene. Their complete component galleries were not interaction-tested.
- **AURA's embedded deployment** visibly said it was temporarily paused. Its catalogue description is evidence, its live experience is unverified.
- **ThreeUI/HorizonX pages** are catalogue wrappers with varied embedded preview/loading states. Several screenshots show still previews, not complete live animations. Do not describe wrapper typography as the underlying template's UX.
- **21st homepage** initially displayed loading tiles; template pages supplied more useful evidence than its first viewport.
- **NOVARA** was visited for inventory completeness and remains excluded because the user previously rejected its direction.

Third-party page screenshots are local audit evidence and intentionally not bundled as app assets or published in this repo. Current-app before screenshots also exist locally. Claude should produce its own original after screenshots from the actual implementation. No historical Folded Signal branding/assets were copied from the separate ShaOn website project.

## Synthesis

Use Hitodama's focus, Space UI's stateful companion idea, Glass Portfolio's material study, Zajno's continuity principles and Supaste's compact utility hierarchy. Build an original product around them. Prioritize a successful first task, one visible primary action, discoverable tools and an avatar that really updates everywhere. Put cinematic treatment in Studio/companion, not every form or routine action. The implementation brief translates these into screen, state, performance and acceptance requirements.
